About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple place to capture thoughts, reminders, and ideas before they disappear. Add a note whenever inspiration strikes and keep your list close at hand.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open the Home page.
  2. Enter your thought in the New note field.
  3. Select Add Note to add it to your notes list.

Features

  • Quickly capture ideas in a simple note form.
  • See your notes together in one list.
  • Use keyboard shortcuts to work more efficiently.

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Tab Move to the next interactive element.
Enter Submit the focused form.
